# Research Sources - Cold Outreach Pipeline for B2B SaaS

## Why I chose this topic

100Hires operates in B2B recruiting — which means cold 
outreach isn't just a marketing tactic here, it's the 
core of how the business grows. I wanted to understand 
how the best people in this space actually build pipeline, 
not just what the textbooks say.

I spent time going through LinkedIn, YouTube, and podcasts 
to find people who are actively doing this — not just 
writing about it. Here's who I found and why I think 
their content is worth studying.

---

## 1. Jason Bay
- **Platform:** YouTube + LinkedIn + Podcast
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/jasondbay
- **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/@BlissfulProspecting
- **Website:** https://blissfulprospecting.com

Jason runs Blissful Prospecting and is probably the most 
consistent voice on cold email I found. What stood out 
to me was his weekly LinkedIn series where he takes a 
real cold email someone received, tears it apart, and 
asks his audience what they'd fix. It's messy and honest 
in a way that feels very different from polished 
"guru" content.

His clients include Zoom, CBRE, and Medallia — so he's 
clearly working with real sales teams, not just coaching 
individuals. He teaches something called the REPLY 
framework which I want to dig into more for the playbook.

**Why he made the list:** Real frameworks, real clients, 
posts every week without fail.

## 2. Armand Farrokh
- **Platform:** YouTube + Podcast + Newsletter
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/armand-farrokh
- **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/@30MPC
- **Website:** https://30mpc.com

I already knew about 30 Minutes to President's Club but 
hadn't dug into Armand's background properly until this 
research. He grew a SaaS company from $0 to $13M ARR 
as VP of Sales — that's not a coaching background, that's 
an operator background. Big difference.

He also co-wrote Cold Calling Sucks (And That's Why It 
Works) which is now a bestseller. The podcast is #1 in 
sales globally which means a lot of practitioners are 
listening to and validating what he says.

**Why he made the list:** Operator with real revenue 
numbers, not just a content creator.

## 3. Nick Cegelski
- **Platform:** YouTube + Podcast
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/nickcegelski
- **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/@30MPC

Nick is Armand's co-founder at 30MPC and the yang to

---

## 4. Josh Braun
- **Platform:** LinkedIn + Newsletter
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/joshbraun
- **Newsletter:** Inbox Better

Josh was one of the more interesting finds. Most cold 
outreach content focuses on tactics — subject lines, 
templates, sequences. Josh focuses on psychology — why 
do people say no, what creates resistance, how do you 
remove friction instead of pushing harder.

His LinkedIn posts are short, sharp, and feel very 
different from the typical sales content. I think his 
perspective will add a layer to the playbook that the 
others don't cover.

**Why he made the list:** Unique angle on buyer 
psychology that most outreach content completely ignores.

---

## 5. Will Aitken
- **Platform:** LinkedIn + YouTube
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/will-aitken
- **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/@WillAitken

Will focuses on making cold outreach sound human rather 
than robotic. A lot of cold email advice ends up feeling 
like a template even when it tries not to — Will is 
specifically focused on fixing that problem.

He collaborates regularly with Jason Bay which shows 
he's part of the actual practitioner community, not 
just a content creator on the sidelines.

**Why he made the list:** Specializes in the thing most 
outreach gets wrong — sounding like a real person.

## 6. Jack Reamer
- **Platform:** Podcast + LinkedIn
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/jackreamer
- **Podcast:** Cold Outreach Podcast
- **Website:** https://salesbread.com

Jack runs SalesBread and co-hosts the Cold Outreach 
Podcast where he breaks down real campaigns with actual 
reply rate data. His positioning is "1 lead per day" 
through LinkedIn and email combined — which is a very 
specific, testable claim that tells me he's measuring 
real results.

He was a slightly less obvious find — I didn't see him 
in the first few Google results which made me more 
confident he's worth including.

**Why he made the list:** Real campaign data, less 
mainstream which means fresher insights.

---

## 7. Nick Abraham
- **Platform:** YouTube + LinkedIn
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/nickabraham12
- **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/@nickabraham

Nick runs a cold email agency and posts very tactical 
content — not motivational, not theoretical. He shares 
actual open rates, reply rates, and what's working right 
now in 2025-2026. Cold email deliverability has changed 
a lot recently with Gmail and Microsoft tightening 
rules, and Nick talks about that specifically.

**Why he made the list:** Current, data-driven, agency 
owner sharing live campaign results.

## 8. Collin Cadmus
- **Platform:** YouTube + Podcast + LinkedIn
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/collincadmus
- **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/@CollinCadmus

Collin is a former VP of Sales who has actually built 
outbound sales teams from scratch at multiple SaaS 
companies. His content comes from that operator 
perspective — what decisions do you make when building 
a pipeline from zero, not just how do you write a 
better subject line.

**Why he made the list:** Brings the "build the whole 
system" perspective that the others don't fully cover.

---

## 9. Morgan J Ingram
- **Platform:** LinkedIn + YouTube
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/morganjingram
- **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/@MorganJIngram

Morgan trains SDR and BDR teams at SaaS companies and 
focuses specifically on multi-channel outreach — 
combining email, LinkedIn, and phone into one sequence 
rather than treating them separately. I found his 
content useful because it bridges the gap between 
individual tactics and a full outreach system.

**Why he made the list:** Multi-channel focus adds 
depth to the playbook beyond just cold email.

---

## 10. Florin Tatulea
- **Platform:** LinkedIn
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/florintatulea

Florin was the most deliberate non-obvious pick on this 
list. He shares A/B test results and reply rate data 
from live outbound campaigns — the kind of granular, 
numbers-first content that's hard to find. Less 
well-known than everyone else here, but that's kind of 
the point. If this list was just the top 10 Google 
results for "cold email experts" it wouldn't be very 
useful research.

**Why he made the list:** Data-driven, underrated, and 
represents the kind of practitioner who tests 
everything rather than assuming.
