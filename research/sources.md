# Research Sources - Cold Outreach Pipeline for B2B SaaS

## Why I chose this topic

100Hires works in B2B recruiting, which means cold
outreach is not just a tactic here — it is how the
business actually grows. I wanted to understand how
people in this space build pipeline in practice, not
just what guides and articles say.

I went through LinkedIn, YouTube, and podcasts to find
people who are actively doing this work. Here is who
I found and why I think their content is worth studying.

---

## 1. Jason Bay

- **Platform:** YouTube + LinkedIn + Podcast
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/jasondbay
- **YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCZKfJ-U8ARc6nGdO_Kg-5sQ/videos
- **Website:** https://outboundsquad.com

Jason runs Outbound Squad (previously Blissful Prospecting) and posts weekly on
LinkedIn where he takes real cold emails and breaks
down what works and what does not. His content is
practical and focused on day-to-day outreach.

His clients include Zoom, CBRE, and Medallia. He also
teaches the REPLY framework for structuring cold emails.

**Why he made the list:** Shares practical cold email advice backed by real client work.

## Selection Criteria

I was not looking for the largest follower counts.

To make this list, an expert needed to meet at least
one of the following criteria:

- Currently running outbound campaigns
- Leading sales or SDR teams
- Sharing real campaign results and data
- Publishing repeatable frameworks
- Creating educational content on cold outreach

I also wanted a mix of perspectives. Some experts focus
on email, some on psychology, some on multi-channel
outreach, and others on building outbound systems.
