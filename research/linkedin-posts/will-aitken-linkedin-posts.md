SELLERS!! Spearfish don't trawl.

If you've selected your target well, know your buyers, and have worked with people just like them, being specific won't limit your outreach — it will help you stand out.

Instead of: "Many teams are spending too much time managing their product content"

Try: "Beverage brands like Coca-Cola told me their ecom team was spending 40 hours a week copying nutritional facts into online marketplaces, which led to a 2-week launch delay costing $235k in missed selling days."

That sounds like someone credible.

## Post 2
**Date:** 3 weeks ago

"Should I personalise or automate my outbound?"

The healthy middle ground: do the prep long before thinking about the outreach.

Almost every cold email is just: "I saw {this}, which usually means {problem}. Curious if that's something you're dealing with?"

Build lists around signals that actually imply the problem your product solves. Instead of one giant useless ICP list you end up with 20+ segments built around specific situations and responsibilities.

That's how teams can automate at scale without abusing obvious mail merges.

## Post 3
**Date:** 2 weeks ago

Do you know what your buyers get fired for?

If your cold email or cold call doesn't connect to a number they grow or protect, you're going to struggle.

Your buyer isn't lying awake thinking about your product. They're thinking about whether their numbers will be green or red by the next QBR.

Learn, write, and ask about that — not what you offer.
