If your cold email response rates are dropping, it might not be your copy.

Cold email is easy. Easy to send. Easy to automate. Easy to scale.

Which means everyone uses it.

The easier the channel, the more crowded it becomes.

That's why there are fewer people cold calling. And even fewer sending direct mail.

More effort. Less competition.

## Post 3
**Date:** 2 weeks ago

Here's how to improve cold outreach response rates.

Make the list do more of the work. See how close you can get to level 9.

Level 1: Contact everyone.
Level 5: Contact Directors of BD with 20+ reps who make cold calls.
Level 9: Contact Directors of BD with 20+ reps who make cold calls, are actively hiring, use Outreach or Salesloft, and have posted about coaching in the last 30 days.

At this point, you're not pushing an idea. You're joining an existing conversation.

Let relevance carry the weight.
